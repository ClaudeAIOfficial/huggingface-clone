THE NEXT STEP FOR COMPANION ROBOTICS

Meet NVIDIA Pets.

Small, expressive robots inspired by animals. Designed to move through real spaces, react to their surroundings, and someday become familiar companions at home.

Watch the concept film ↗

Meet the companions

Two distinct robotic designs share the same clean white-and-orange industrial language. Each has a different body, movement concept and personality, built around what a physical device could realistically express.

MODEL 01

Robotic Penguin

Rounded optical head, articulated flippers, a compact upright body and small orange feet. Designed around expressive head movements and a friendly, controlled waddle.

MODEL 02

Robotic Turtle

A stable four-legged platform with a segmented shell and mobile neck. Designed around slow, deliberate movement, observation and a calm personality.

Built for everyday spaces

Imagine a small robotic companion that can look toward you when you speak, take a few careful steps closer, or wait nearby while you work. NVIDIA Pets explores that kind of embodied interaction through a pair of approachable animal-inspired designs.

01Expressive movement

Small head turns, gentle gestures and distinct movement patterns.

02Perception

Future vision and proximity sensing for physical-world interaction.

03Personality

Individual behaviors and optional voice-based companionship.

04Modular construction

Rounded shells, compact actuators and replaceable hardware elements.

From concept to companion

The current stage contains industrial design renders, visual demonstrations and preliminary behavior ideas. It does not include manufactured robot units or validated autonomy. The next engineering steps would be mechanical prototypes, electronic control, gait testing and safety evaluation.

Development status: These are proposed physical robot companions. The current images and video use CGI, not footage of working robots. The project does not claim an official NVIDIA product launch.
Design and engineeringTechnical direction

Hardware architecture

The two robots are conceived as small, self-contained physical devices. Their shells, joints, sensor positions, and feet follow a consistent industrial design system, while their locomotion mechanisms differ.

Mechanical systems

The penguin concept calls for stable low-speed bipedal movement and mechanically limited flipper gestures. The turtle concept favors a low center of gravity, short steps and an articulating neck. Both would require motion-control work and physical testing.

Sensing and autonomy

Future versions could use cameras, microphones, proximity sensors, and embedded controllers to recognize interaction cues, avoid nearby objects and respond through movement. These subsystems remain proposed features, not demonstrated hardware capabilities.

Safety and buildability

A functioning product would require torque limits, fall detection, obstacle avoidance, safe charging, thermal design and durability testing. The design aims to make moving parts simple and accessible, but there are no published mechanical performance results yet.

About NVIDIA PetsProject notes

Robot companionship

NVIDIA Pets is an industrial design exploration into animal-inspired physical robot companions, beginning with a penguin and a turtle. The project emphasizes approachable form, expressive motion and the possibility of embodied personal AI.

The current video and renders are concept visualizations. There are no claimed manufactured units, verified hardware demonstrations or official NVIDIA release associated with this independent project.
